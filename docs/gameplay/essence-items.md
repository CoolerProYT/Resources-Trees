# Essence Items

Essences are crafted materials for resources that have no single vanilla block to use, like mob drops or the elements. They are the sapling material for their resource type: a Zombie Oak Sapling is crafted from Zombie Essence around an Oak Sapling.

## All essences

All essence recipes are shapeless, so the order of the ingredients doesn't matter.

## Looks

Every essence uses the same base texture, tinted with its own colour, so they share a look while staying easy to tell apart.
