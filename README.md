# 🌳 ResourcesTrees
**Grow your resources instead of mining them!**

ResourcesTrees introduces a brand-new way to obtain materials in Minecraft: **by farming resource-yielding trees**. Instead of spending hours digging through caves, you can now cultivate specialized trees that drop valuable resources.

---

## ⚡ Features
- 🌱 **Data-Driven System** – Easily add your own custom resource trees through datapacks. Perfect for modpack creators and players who want to expand gameplay.
- 🪓 **Sustainable Resource Gathering** – Chop trees, replant, and keep your resource farm growing.
- 🛠 **Highly Customizable** – Define your own tree types, loot, and progression paths to fit your pack’s theme or difficulty.
- 🌍 **Seamless Integration** – Works great in vanilla-style survival, modpacks, and skyblock-like worlds.

---

## 📖 Getting Started
1. Craft a resource sapling with crafting table
2. Plant a resource sapling.
3. Grow it into a tree.
4. Harvest valuable drops!
5. Replant and expand your farm.

Modpack developers can add their own tree definitions through datapacks, making **ResourcesTrees** extremely flexible and easy to integrate.

---

## 🧩 Compatibility
- Designed for **vanilla-style gameplay**.
- Works in **modded packs** and **skyblock maps**.
- Fully **data-driven** – no code required to add new trees.
